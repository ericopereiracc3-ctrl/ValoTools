Privacy and transparency

Privacy Policy

This policy explains the limited information involved when you use ValoTools.

Our current approach

ValoTools is designed to work without collecting personal information directly from you.

  • No user accounts
  • No contact forms or newsletter
  • No payments
  • No analytics or advertising
  • No affiliate links

Language preference

ValoTools stores only your language preference in your browser's localStorage using the key valotools-language. You can remove it at any time by deleting this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting and fonts

ValoTools is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information, including IP addresses, for security and service integrity purposes.

Fonts are currently loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those files, Google may receive technical connection data such as your IP address.

Sale and commercial sharing

ValoTools does not sell personal data or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.
